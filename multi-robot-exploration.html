Research · IFRoS master’s thesis

Decentralized exploration & semantic inspection

Coordinating multiple UAVs to explore an unknown environment and inspect targets of interest.

Master’s thesis researcherMulti-UAV systems · Planning

Challenge

A team of robots exploring an unknown space needs to coordinate where it goes and decide what to inspect as its map develops.

My contribution

My master’s thesis at the University of Zagreb’s LARICS lab developed and evaluated a graph-based approach to decentralized exploration and semantic inspection.

Approach

The approach uses graph-based planning for exploration and coordinates inspection tasks across multiple UAVs as the environment is mapped.

Outcome

The work was published at the 2025 International Conference on Unmanned Aircraft Systems. The demonstration shows the experimental workflow.

Read the IEEE paper

Tools & methods

UAVs, Graph-based planning, Multi-robot coordination, Semantic inspection
